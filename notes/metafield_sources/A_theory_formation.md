## What this strand covers and what it does not

**Covered.**
- **The enterprise itself is an established field.** Automated theory formation runs from AM (1976) to HR (1997–2014). Theory exploration was named by Buchberger in 2000, had its own Automatheo workshops (2009–2012), and continues in the QuickSpec, Hipster, IsaCoSy and TheSy line. Current ML work (FERMAT, Minimo) calls open-ended discovery of new theories a grand challenge. So claim (a) does not hold as stated. Something like "a new direction inside automated theory formation" can be defended.
- **The loop and the setting are not new.** Prover plus finite-model-finder over finite-algebra axioms, with conjectures filtered by counter-models, is HR's loop (OTTER/MACE, 1999). HR already ran on quasigroups, QG-quasigroups, loops and star algebras, and it led to a theorem about a whole one-axiom family.
- **Scoring by interest has a literature.** Interestingness measures were surveyed by Colton, Bundy and Walsh (2000). Ranking by "short statement, hard proof" appears in Charnley and Colton (2008) and again in an LLM paper (2026).
- **Search over theories has precedents, so (b) is only partly new.** Charnley and Colton generated 100 specialisations of a base algebra and ranked them by average proof length. TheoryMine generated many new theories, filtered them against a known library, and required each theorem to use every function of its theory, which is close to the project's bridge and synergy rules.
- **Fusing two theories has a formal precedent.** COINVENT's theory blending (colimits, emergent axioms) gives one, though only on hand-built examples.

**Not covered (nothing found in this strand).**
- **The search itself.** I found no open-ended, quality-diversity or evolutionary search over *sets of laws in two operations* at the scale of millions of evaluations. Nor did I find "synergy" defined as laws implied by the whole theory but by no single law, used as the objective.
- **Claim (c).** No taxonomy of *theories*, rather than of models, by one-operation reduct signatures. No use of species accumulation curves or Chao1 on discovered theory families: one targeted search returned only ecology papers.
- **Claim (d).** Conjectures *across* families are not covered. HR, AGInT, MATHsAiD and IsaCoSy conjecture inside one theory. The only cross-theory pattern I found is Charnley and Colton's weak correlation between definition size and theory difficulty.
- **The project's research practice.** Preregistration and a contamination-free, LLM-free scoring loop do not appear in this literature.

**Caveats.** Absence here is weak evidence. Several rows are snippet-only (Colton 2002, Colton et al. 2000, MCS, the residue-classes paper, Buchberger 2006). Other strands (equational logic and universal algebra, ATP-driven exploration) may hold closer work.
