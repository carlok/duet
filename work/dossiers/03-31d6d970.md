## Literature check

**A non-associative weakening of rectangular skew lattices.** E proves that the laws make both operations idempotent and give x·(y·x) = x = (x·y)·x. With associativity of · added, E proves x·y = y∘x, (x·y)·z = x·z and associativity of ∘. Conversely, a rectangular band with ∘ as its transpose satisfies the four laws (E). So the associative case is exactly a rectangular band with its reverse, which is term-equivalent to the rectangular skew lattices: Leech, *Noncommutative Lattices: Skew Lattices, Skew Boolean Algebras and Beyond* (Univ. of Primorska Press, 2020; Zbl 1530.06001), Prop. 1.3.14. With · = ∧ and ∘ = ∨, laws 1 and 2 are Leech's absorption identities a∧(a∨b) = a and (b∨a)∧a = a. Every class in that book assumes associativity. Without associativity, ∘ is a "connector": x·(x∘y) = x and (x∘y)·y = y. Models: Zelinka's anticommutative conservative groupoids (Math. Bohem. 1994, doi:10.21136/MB.1994.126168, review only), each with ∘ its transpose (hand check), and others where ∘ is a different connector, such as the smallest model, where 0∘2 = 1 but 2·0 = 2. Translations cannot be injective in a non-degenerate model: an injective left translation gives y·x = x. So the rack, cycle-set and Yang–Baxter literature does not apply. Checked and not matching (reviews or partial reads by a web-only agent; key claims rechecked here):
- Płonka's diagonal algebras (rectangular bands in the binary case);
- Crvenković–Dudek rectangular groupoids, (xy)z = xz;
- Boykett's rectangular groupoids (arXiv:1108.1658);
- Dudek's medial modes.

Not read: Padmanabhan, Algebra Universalis 1971 (doi:10.1007/BF02944996); Evans, "Products of points". No source found for the full theory. It stands to rectangular skew lattices as Fried's weakly associative lattices stand to lattices, but that analogy is ours.
